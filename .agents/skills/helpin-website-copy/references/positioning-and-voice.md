# Positioning and voice

Use this reference for editorial direction, not release facts. The central story is that customer history improves the next action, the products provide places to act, and the team controls the process.

## What Helpin leads with

These are owner-confirmed messages as of September 2026. Check the product-truth record before reusing a qualifier, because plans and releases change.

1. **One customer history, with agents that act on it.** Support, Projects, CRM, Meetings, and Knowledge share one history, and AI agents use it to answer, plan, and follow up.
2. **Open source, on Helpin Cloud or your own servers.** Name both editions wherever hosting, pricing, trust, or comparisons come up: Helpin Cloud, hosted by Helpin, and the self-hosted Community edition under AGPL-3.0. Don't mention one without the other.
3. **For support buyers, four advantages lead:**
   - **No per-seat or per-resolution fees.** One price per workspace with unlimited teammates; every Cloud plan includes an AI usage allowance, and overage is metered only if a paid plan turns it on. Self-hosting has no license fee.
   - **Bring your own AI models.** Always when self-hosted; on Helpin Cloud, on the Enterprise plan. Every other Cloud plan includes AI usage.
   - **Docs that keep up with the product.** Agents draft help-center updates from support conversations and from the code changes you ship. Nothing goes live until your team publishes it.
   - **From ticket to fix to customer.** Agents can turn a ticket into a task, have a coding agent open the fix as a pull request, and tell the customer when it ships. Your team chooses which steps need approval.

Other tools have parts of these; Intercom and Zendesk both suggest article updates from support conversations, for example. State what Helpin does and credit the other tool where it's true, rather than claiming others can't. See [claim verification](claim-verification.md).

## Give each layer a job

- History: what was asked, tried, promised, and recorded afterward.
- Products: the inbox, project plan, relationship, meeting record, and published guidance people work with.
- Agents: the specific investigation, plan, proposed change, or follow-up they can prepare.
- Controls: the permitted tools, review boundary, and trigger for the action.

A paragraph does not need to explain all four. Let neighboring sections build the story instead of repeating the platform introduction.

## Editorial preferences

Use natural sentences rather than automatically splitting every headline into two fragments. Use “platform” for broad positioning when appropriate; keep “workspace” where it names an actual UI object, API resource, or verified billing unit. Do not globally replace technical terminology.

Keep Ask Agent recognizable as the coordinator. Introduce a specialist by its job before its name when that helps a visitor understand it. Verify the current roster before listing names or counts.

Preserve explicitly requested removals of captions and navigation filler. Do not restore old demo labels merely because a template contains them. If illustrative disclosure is needed, use an existing suitable location or discuss the conflict in the report rather than adding clutter without authorization.

## Editing examples

These are writing examples, not approved replacement copy or verified claims.

| Weak direction | More concrete direction | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seamlessly unlock customer intelligence. | See what the customer already tried. | Names information the reader can use. |
| One system for everything. | Keep the request attached to the task. | Shows a relationship instead of an absolute promise. |
| Agents know your customers. | Check the earlier report before suggesting a workaround. | Shows how history changes the action. |
| Instantly close every loop. | Prepare an update after the team confirms the release. | Separates preparation from execution. |
| Your docs. Your answers. Your future. | Help customers find the next step. | Gives the heading one clear job. |

Descriptions should add a capability or useful boundary, not repeat the headline using synonyms. Retain necessary qualifiers even when a shorter line looks cleaner.
