# Review checklist

Use before delivery. Mark checks as passed, failed, not run, or not applicable; do not treat a template as evidence.

- Mode matches the request; draft/audit did not mutate website source.
- Current route, source, target release, and relevant neighboring page were identified.
- Section order, IDs, cards, tabs, FAQs, anchors, and demo states match the inventory.
- Every authorized text slot is covered, including a11y labels and hidden states.
- Headlines are understandable; descriptions add information; repeated sections have distinct jobs.
- House style holds: US English, Oxford comma, curly quotes, sentence case, headline periods, no exclamation marks, CTA labels from the style sheet.
- Hosting, pricing, and comparison copy names both Helpin Cloud and the self-hosted Community edition.
- Competitor statements come from their own current documentation, credit similar features, and use names only, with no logos or badges.
- Product depth remains visible; agents are neither a vague promise nor forced into every headline.
- Ask Agent and specialists have distinct roles; the roster was not assumed from an old draft.
- Material claims have scoped evidence; unknowns/conflicts are in the report, not customer-facing placeholders.
- Prices, trial, billing unit, limits, beta labels, and licenses were not guessed or silently changed.
- Demos consistently identify OrbitDesk and Northstar; history changes an actual decision.
- Existing tasks are reused; approval, execution, release, notification, publication, and indexing remain distinct.
- Identity, tool access, and account authorization are not conflated.
- CTA labels match destinations and offers; broken links are reported accurately.
- No unsolicited section, global copy change, schema, dependency, or behavior change was introduced.
- Interpolation, localization, keys, instrumentation, and animation timing remain intact.
- Applicable existing checks ran; desktop/mobile wrapping and changed states were inspected when available.
- Evidence distinguishes build results from visual inspection and product verification.
- Progress was updated only when authorized; approval has an explicit human source.
- Final delivery gives one recommended version or the scoped changes, actual checks, and remaining blockers.
