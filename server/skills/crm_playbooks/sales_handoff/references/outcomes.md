# Acceptance and follow-through rules

- Completion requires explicit receiving-owner acceptance of a usable handoff.
  A drafted handoff, created Doc, assigned task or successful run is insufficient.
- Preserve sales accountability and the history of reassignment until acceptance.
  Do not infer acceptance from silence, a task status or model confidence.
- Onboarding and first value are separately tracked objectives. An accepted handoff
  does not establish that the customer is onboarded or has achieved first value.
- Missing CSM ownership, ambiguous sold commitments or customer unreadiness need
  scoped escalation, not an ownerless task list or invented schedule.
- Waiting for acceptance needs a responsible party and checkpoint/expiry. Routine
  checks do not reset overdue work. Repeated rejections need changed evidence or
  explicit direction before a materially equivalent proposal is recreated.
