# Recovery and follow-through rules

- Risk resolution and confirmed renewal are distinct milestones. Each needs
  authoritative evidence or an explicitly labeled human assessment.
- A closed Support ticket or PM task can prompt reassessment; it does not prove
  that the customer agrees the problem is resolved or intends to renew.
- Silence is not consent. A changed renewal date or new customer objection can
  invalidate a pending action and requires revalidation before further contact.
- A wait has an owner and checkpoint/expiry. Unchanged checks do not reset overdue
  commitments, approval expiry or the time since actual customer progress.
- Uncertain message delivery requires reconciliation, not a blind resend. Repeated
  no-progress work or unavailable capabilities require a visible blocker and
  escalation, not unlimited attempts or silently abandoning the renewal.
