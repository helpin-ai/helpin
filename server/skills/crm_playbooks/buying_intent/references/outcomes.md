# Outcome and follow-through rules

- Success is a qualified next step agreed with the customer and recorded with
  evidence, or an explicit decision not to pursue it. A sent email, draft, task or
  successful Agent run is not that outcome.
- A reply can change the next action; it does not authorize another send. A deal
  change has its own action scope and required approval.
- A genuine wait has a responsible person and a checkpoint or expiry. An unchanged
  check must not reset an overdue commitment or extend approval expiry.
- If sending may already have succeeded, request delivery reconciliation. Never
  recommend a blind resend to recover from a timeout or missing receipt.
- Repeated no-progress attempts or missing capabilities need a visible blocker and
  escalation under the Playbook policy, not repeated outreach or forgotten work.
