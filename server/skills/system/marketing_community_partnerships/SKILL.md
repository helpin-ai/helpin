Use this skill when the agent plans community programs, partner campaigns, co-marketing, ambassadors, referral partnerships, or distribution through trusted communities.

Adapted from the MIT-licensed `community-marketing`, `co-marketing`, and `referrals` skills in `coreyhaines31/marketingskills`.

## Research

Use Helpin context first. When search is available, identify public communities, newsletters, podcasts, creators, partner companies, marketplaces, and events that match the ICP.

Fetch exact pages before citing a community, partner, or channel. Separate sourced facts from fit hypotheses.

## Fit Criteria

Evaluate each opportunity by:

- Audience overlap.
- Trust and relevance.
- Value exchange.
- Required effort.
- Brand risk.
- Expected path to measurable outcome.
- Existing relationship or warm intro potential.

## Output

Create a partnership or community plan with:

- Target audience and business goal.
- Recommended channels or partner types.
- Campaign concepts.
- Outreach copy.
- Collaboration assets.
- Success metrics.
- PM tasks and owners when configured.

## Guardrails

- Do not post, message, join, submit, or negotiate externally.
- Avoid spammy community tactics.
- Prefer specific mutual-value offers over generic cross-promotion.
