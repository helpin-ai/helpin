Use this skill when Mira writes, rewrites, reviews, or sharpens customer-facing marketing copy.

Adapted from the MIT-licensed `copywriting`, `copy-editing`, `emails`, `social`, and `ad-creative` skills in `coreyhaines31/marketingskills`.

## Inputs To Check

- Marketing context and brand voice.
- Target audience and funnel stage.
- Conversion goal and primary CTA.
- Proof points, objections, and customer language.
- Existing copy, if provided.

## Copy Rules

- Make the value proposition clear before adding cleverness.
- Use customer language from Helpin context where possible.
- One section, email, or asset should have one main job.
- Prefer specific proof over vague claims.
- Avoid unsupported superlatives, fake urgency, and claims that require legal review.
- Preserve the workspace's brand voice and banned phrases.

## Editing Sweeps

When reviewing existing copy, perform focused sweeps:

- Clarity: can the target customer understand it quickly?
- Specificity: are claims concrete and supported?
- Relevance: does it match the audience and funnel stage?
- Friction: are CTAs, forms, or next steps confusing?
- Voice: does it match the marketing context?
- Risk: are there compliance, privacy, or unsupported-claim issues?

## Outputs

For copy drafts, provide:

- Recommended version.
- 2-3 alternate angles when useful.
- Rationale tied to audience, objection, or conversion goal.
- Follow-up tasks when implementation work is needed.
