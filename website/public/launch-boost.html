Help boost the launch post

Need a hand on the launch post. Three things help, in this order. A like does almost nothing. Do every step in each one.

1. Quote it

On your phone

  1. Open the post.
  2. Tap the repost icon (two arrows).
  3. Tap Quote, not Repost.

  4. Write one real line. Who it is for, or why you would use it. Not “great launch.”

    0 / 280

  5. Paste your line, then post.

On desktop

Same icon, then Quote.

A quote puts the post in front of your followers with your name on it. A blank repost barely counts.

2. Reply

  1. Open the post.
  2. Tap the reply icon.

  3. Write one specific line. A question, a use case, or who you would send it to.

    0 / 280

  4. Paste your line, then reply.

I will reply to every one. That back-and-forth is what keeps it moving. If we follow each other, your reply counts for more.

3. Copy the link the right way

This only counts if you use the button. Copying from the address bar does not.

On your phone

  1. Open the post.
  2. Tap the share icon.
  3. Tap Copy link. Not the iPhone or Android share sheet.
  4. Paste that link into WhatsApp, iMessage, Slack, or Telegram.

On desktop

  1. Click the share icon on the post.
  2. Click Copy link.
  3. Paste it.

X never sees the chat. It only sees that Copy link tap, and a later open of that link.
